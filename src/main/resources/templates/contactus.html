Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start selling on CampusCart?

Simply sign up with your CUET email, complete your profile, and click "Sell Item" to list your products. It's that easy!

Is CampusCart only for CUET students?

Yes, CampusCart is exclusively designed for CUET students. You need a valid CUET student email to register.

How do I contact a seller?

Once you add an item to your cart or wishlist, you can view the seller's contact information and reach out directly.
